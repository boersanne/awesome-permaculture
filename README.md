# Awesome Permaculture

Curated readings, designs, and tooling for permaculture — the ecology-led design discipline for human habitats. Heavy on free / open resources.

## Foundational books

- *Permaculture: A Designers' Manual* (Bill Mollison) — the encyclopedic reference. Dense, opinionated, indispensable.
- *Gaia's Garden* (Toby Hemenway) — best entry point for home-scale design.
- *The Earth Care Manual* (Patrick Whitefield) — temperate-climate adaptation of Mollison.
- *Restoration Agriculture* (Mark Shepard) — perennial-polyculture commercial farming.
- *The One-Straw Revolution* (Masanobu Fukuoka) — natural-farming roots; not strictly permaculture but adjacent.

## Course material (free)

- [Permaculture Design Course (Geoff Lawton)](https://www.geofflawton.com/) — paid, but his free YouTube content covers most of the syllabus.
- [Open Permaculture School](https://www.permaculturedesigntraining.com/) — free PDC modules.
- [Toby Hemenway's lectures on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=toby+hemenway) — multi-hour archive.

## Plant & species references

- [Plants For A Future (PFAF)](https://pfaf.org/) — searchable database of edible/useful plants. Free for individuals.
- [Practical Plants](http://practicalplants.org/) — community wiki, similar dataset.
- [Useful Temperate Plants Database](http://temperate.theferns.info/) — Ken Fern's curated list.
- [USDA PLANTS Database](https://plants.usda.gov/) — authoritative US flora reference.

## Design tools

- [crop-rotation-planner](https://github.com/boersanne/crop-rotation-planner) — multi-year rotation in the browser.
- [QGIS](https://qgis.org/) — free GIS for site mapping and overlays.
- [SketchUp Free](https://www.sketchup.com/plans-and-pricing/sketchup-free) — quick 3D site sketches.
- [Permapeople](https://permapeople.org/) — plant database + community garden planner.

## Climate & site data

- [WorldClim](https://www.worldclim.org/) — global gridded climate data, free.
- [SoilGrids](https://soilgrids.org/) — soil properties at 250m resolution.
- [USDA Web Soil Survey](https://websoilsurvey.nrcs.usda.gov/) — US-only, very detailed.
- [ECMWF reanalysis](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/dataset/ecmwf-reanalysis-v5) — historical weather.
